## Problem

The belt reductions in my robot arm had too much backlash.

## Solution

A parametric cycloidal disc model in SolidWorks (roller count, eccentricity and diameter as variables), FEA stress analysis and a PETG prototype with needle bearings.

## Results

Backlash below 0.2° and a 1:14 ratio in an 80 mm diameter housing.
